# Medicine Distribution

> Original guide by Frozen_Heart (mod author), posted 13 Jun on the Steam Workshop discussions of
> *Extensive Health Rework Evolved* (workshop id 3726328119). Archived verbatim on 2026-10-05.
> Source: https://steamcommunity.com/workshop/filedetails/discussion/3726328119/565911090118371159/

EHR medications use Project Zomboid loot tables, so exact results are still random and affected by sandbox loot settings. Most medication packages spawn with a random amount of remaining doses. Pharmacies are more likely to contain fuller packages; non-pharmacy locations usually have partially used supplies.

Best Places To Find Basic Medicine

Pharmacies / Drugstores
Good source for common and mid-tier medication:
- Cold & Flu Tablets
- Antipyretic Tablets
- Cough Syrup
- Cough Suppressant
- Anti-Nausea Tablets
- Anti-Diarrheal
- Anti-Inflammatory Pills
- Antiseptic Cream
- Muscle Relaxants
- Electrolyte Powder
- Bronchodilator Inhaler
- Instant Ice Packs
- Topical Permethrin
- Antipsychotics
- Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonists
- Clean Bandages / Alcoholic Bandages
- Rare Saline Bags

Supermarkets / GigaMart / General Store Medical Shelves

Lighter medical loot, mostly OTC supplies:
- Cold / fever / cough medicine
- Anti-nausea and anti-diarrheal medicine
- Anti-inflammatory pills
- Electrolyte powder
- Antiseptic cream
- Clean bandages
- Instant ice packs
- Topical permethrin

Houses
Very rare medical loot can appear in medicine cabinets, bedroom furniture, shelves, desks, and similar containers:
- Basic OTC medication
- Antiseptic cream
- Muscle relaxants
- Nitric oxide boosters
- Rare leftover prescription medication, if enabled in sandbox settings

Medical Locations

Clinics / Medical Offices / Waiting Rooms

Better chance for prescription medication and medical supplies:
- Prescription Antibiotics
- Antiviral Capsules
- Antifungal Tablets
- Oral Rehydration Kit
- Antibiotic Ointment
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics
- Furosemide
- Antipsychotics
- Dual Orexin Receptor Antagonists
- Syringes
- Saline Bags
- Clean Bandages / Alcoholic Bandages
- Topical Permethrin
- Blood Bags and Empty Blood Bags, rarely

Medical Storage / Hospital Storage
Best civilian source for stronger medication:
- Activated Charcoal
- Antiparasitic Pills
- Tetanus Antitoxin
- TB Antibiotics
- IV Kit
- IV Fluids
- Saline Bags
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics
- Rare clinical medication such as IV Antibiotics, IV Ciprofloxacin, Corticosteroid Injection, Respiratory Support Kit, and Emergency Sepsis Kit

Large Hospital Storage
Best non-military source for clinical-grade medication:
- IV Antibiotics
- IV Ciprofloxacin
- IV Metronidazole
- IV Vancomycin
- IV Amphotericin
- IV Fluids
- Emergency Sepsis Kit
- Corticosteroid Injection
- Respiratory Support Kit
- Chelation Kit
- Albendazole Injection
- Tetanus Immunoglobulin
- IV Kits
- Syringes
- Saline Bags
- Blood Bags

Emergency Sources

First Aid Kits

Mostly basic field medicine:
- Antiseptic Cream
- Anti-Inflammatory Pills
- Antipyretic Tablets
- Syringes
- Clean Bandages
- Alcoholic Bandages
- Instant Ice Packs
- Topical Permethrin
- Rare Nitric Oxide Booster

Ambulances

Glove boxes and front seats contain basic supplies. Ambulance trunks are much better and can contain:
- Bandages and disinfecting supplies
- Anti-inflammatory / fever / nausea medicine
- Electrolyte powder
- Bronchodilator inhalers
- Activated charcoal
- Antibiotic ointment
- Oral Rehydration Kit
- Prescription and Broad Spectrum Antibiotics
- IV Kits, Syringes, Saline Bags, IV Fluids
- Respiratory Support Kit
- Corticosteroid Injection
- Emergency Sepsis Kit
- Rare IV antibiotics and other clinical medication

Medical Bags / Medical Satchels
Portable medical bags can contain a strong mix of basic and prescription supplies, with a small chance for rare clinical medication.

Military / Police / Tactical Loot

High-value medical loot:
- IV Antibiotics
- IV Ciprofloxacin
- IV Fluids
- Emergency Sepsis Kit
- Corticosteroid Injection
- Respiratory Support Kit
- Tetanus Immunoglobulin
- IV Kits
- Syringes
- Saline Bags
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics
- Blood Bags

Gun Stores / Police Stations / Police Vehicles

Combat Stimulants are restricted tactical supplies. They can be found rarely in:
- Gun stores
- Military storage
- Police storage
- Police bags
- Police vehicles

Blood Supplies

Blood Bags, Empty Blood Bags, Saline Bags, and IV Fluids are most common in:
- Hospital medical storage
- Military medical storage
- Ambulances
- Clinics, rarely

Blood bag types follow realistic rarity: O+ is the most common, AB- is the rarest.

Knox / Experimental Items

Experimental Knox-related items only spawn in the Military Research Facility basements, if enabled in sandbox settings:
- Phalanx Suppressant Pills
- Antibody Test Kit
- Immunobooster Shot
- Gene Therapy Kit, extremely rare and found deeper underground

Flyers and Recipes
Disease flyers and medicine recipe guides spawn mostly in:
- Medical storage
- Clinics
- Medical cabinets
- Pharmacies / drugstores
- Rarely in mailboxes

Crafted-Only Medicine
Homemade and herbal medicines are crafted, not normal loot:
- Homemade Activated Charcoal
- Plant-Based Antibiotics
- Homemade Antiseptic Cream
- Homemade Antibiotic Ointment
- Homemade Topical Permethrin
- Homemade Cough Syrup
- Homemade Muscle Relaxant
- Homemade Painkillers
- Homemade Sleeping Pills
- Herbal / Antipyretic / Relaxant Tea
- Spiderweb Bandage
