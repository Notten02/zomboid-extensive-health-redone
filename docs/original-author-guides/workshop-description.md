❗❗❗I'm taking a break from modding for a while - could be 1-2 months, could be 1-2 years. Feel free to modify, fix, edit, whatever. I don't own these mods, the community does.

A small disclaimer: it's probably better to start and redo this mod from scratch, since it has an insane amount of workarounds, performance issues, and legacy code/rewritten parts on top of new additions, which sometimes just can't work together. At first this mod was supposed to be just a small improvement on my own vision of EHR, but it turned into a pretty big expansion.

So again, I strongly recommend starting from scratch if someone decides to support it, because you'll likely waste more time figuring out how or why it even works instead of fixing/adding new stuff. 

This mod expands the medical side of Project Zomboid with new diseases, symptoms, treatments, diagnostic tools, blood-related mechanics, medical knowledge progression, and a more punishing survival experience.

Project Zomboid health is often simple enough: patch the wound, disinfect it, take some pills, hope the bite was not that bite. This mod makes your character’s body feel more fragile, unpredictable, and dangerous to ignore.

 🖥️EHR Health Monitoring 

New health monitoring UI with disease information, active medication and body status.

To open new health panel press H (can be configured in Mod Options, not sandbox)

Vanilla health panel still can be used, check mod options to configure what your primary health panel will be.

EHR panel opens in compact mode by default, you can change that in mod options as well

The mod adds a wide range of new illnesses and medical conditions designed to fit naturally into Project Zomboid’s survival loop.

Diseases can be caused by wounds, corpses, contaminated food or water, poor conditions, temperature exposure, toxins, infection, untreated injuries, mental and other survival-related risks

Some illnesses are mild and manageable. Others can slowly spiral out of control if ignored. Symptoms may overlap, making diagnosis less obvious and forcing the player to pay attention instead of treating every problem like “just take antibiotics.”

For more detailed info of every disease please read pinned topic in discussions

Extensive Health Rework Evolved adds new medical items, medicines, tests, and treatment tools.
Some treatments are designed for specific conditions. Others may reduce symptoms, slow disease progression, or help recovery. Using the wrong treatment may be useless, risky, or simply a waste of supplies.

The mod introduces more detailed medication behavior. Different medicines can affect different symptoms and conditions. Some may help reduce fatigue, endurance penalties, infection risks, or specific disease effects.
Medication can have active states, doses, side effects, durations, and condition-specific usefulness.

The goal is to make treatment feel more intentional instead of turning every illness into a one-button fix.
Some medication can be found in houses, more advanced in clinics and hospitals, and top tier experimental ones in secret research facility and military bases.

New Medical Crafting
New recipes to make survival in wilderness more manageable but it will not replace clinical items.

Medicinal Plant Search
Players with enough medical and foraging knowledge or read the special flyer can inspect suitable bushes for useful plants. Search spots are hidden at first, so you must identify promising signs by hand. Some spots may hide thorns and can scratch your hands unless you wear intact gloves.

Hive & Spiderweb Search
Some trees may contain honeycomb or spiderwebs. Not every tree is useful, and inspected trees cannot be searched again for some time. Move your hand carefully through the tree area, avoiding bees and spiders. Collected honeycomb and spiderwebs are used in medical crafting.

The mod expands blood-related gameplay with new mechanics connected to blood loss, blood types, transfusions, and dangerous complications. Blood is no longer just red decoration on your shirt after missing one fence swing.

Blood level can be tracked by new special medical watches

🪡New Suturing minigame

Vanilla suturing system is really forgiving and does not require player skill or medical skill practically at all. New suturing system adds depths to this mechanic and respect your character's first aid skills. If your suture is not good enough, there is a 40% chance of triggering cellulitis that if untreated will give severe complications. So keep your hands steady and don't rush(unless you bleeding to death and running from zombies. Then cellulitis is the least of your problems)  The more First Aid you have the easier it's become (Can be turned off in sandbox settings, but why?)

🤧Immune System
EHR now includes a dynamic Immune System that reacts to your character’s physical condition. It does not grant complete immunity or cure active diseases. Instead, it slightly increases or decreases the risk of contracting supported diseases.
Your immune condition is displayed in the Immune System tab of the EHR Medical Panel.

📻
The mod includes atmospheric radio content connected to health, survival, and the collapse of the world.
A daily broadcast on 47.0 Mhz features Amelie Crowe, a radio host whose story progresses over time. Her transmissions may include medical advice, survival warnings and personal reflections

9 AM – medical advises which gives small first aid xp
3 PM – Storry progression
9 PM – Useful knowledge of different diseases

The broadcast is intended to add atmosphere, worldbuilding, and a sense that someone out there is still trying to explain the end of everything — badly, beautifully, and probably while running out of supplies.

👩‍⚕️ A new perk with unique skill 
EHR surgeon gives you First Aid 10, special perk that works with scalpel and full knowledge of all diseases

 📑Diagnosis and Medical Knowledge 
Not every condition is immediately obvious.
Press J to open handbook and see what knowledge you have about diseases
The mod gives more value to medical skill, books, and proper diagnosis. Some diseases may share similar symptoms, and the player may need specific knowledge or tools to better understand what is happening.

Specialized medical flyers can help the character identify illnesses, understand symptoms, and choose the correct treatment.
Medical knowledge matters now.
