Most EHR diseases use four stages:
- Stage 1 - early signs or incubation.
- Stage 2 - active illness.
- Stage 3 - peak danger.
- Stage 4 - recovery, late-stage danger, or complication stage depending on the disease.

Some conditions work differently:
- Concussion has 3 stages and starts at Stage 3, then gradually improves.
- Delirium has no normal stages and stays active until treated.
- Insomnia has 3 stages; Stage 3 does not naturally recover.
- Knox Infection is mostly controlled by vanilla zombie infection rules.

Medication notes:
- Relief medicine reduces symptoms but does not always cure the disease.
- Cure medicine must usually be taken as a full course.
- Taking course-based medication too early can waste a dose.
- Strong drugs can cause side effects or overdose if abused.
- Some clinical drugs require a syringe or IV kit.

[NEW] 💊Painkiller Addiction
Cause:
- Repeated use of standard Painkillers builds hidden dependency risk.
- The first four doses within 24 hours add little risk.
- The fifth and sixth doses add significantly more risk.
- Seven or more doses within 24 hours are extremely dangerous.
- Taking another dose less than 3 hours after the previous one adds extra risk.
- Risk begins to decrease after 24 hours without painkillers.

Typical timing:
- There is no fixed incubation period; addiction begins when hidden dependency risk reaches its limit (100).
- Stage 2 begins after 24 hours.
- Stage 3 begins after 48 hours.
- The condition is permanent until properly treated.

Symptoms:
- Cravings for painkillers.
- Increasing stress.
- Increasing unhappiness and depression.
- Increased thirst.
- An active Painkiller or Buprenorphine dose temporarily suppresses withdrawal symptoms.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - mild restlessness, cravings, stress, unhappiness, and thirst.
- Stage 2 - cravings become stronger; stress, unhappiness, and thirst increase significantly.
- Stage 3 - severe dependence; unhappiness can reach its maximum, with very high stress and increased thirst. This stage persists indefinitely.

Treatment:
- Buprenorphine cures Painkiller Addiction through a 120-hour treatment course.
- The course requires 10 doses, with one dose every 12 hours.
- Each active Buprenorphine dose suppresses withdrawal symptoms.
- Regular Painkillers also suppress symptoms temporarily, but do not cure the addiction.

Prevention:
- Avoid taking five or more Painkiller doses within 24 hours.
- Never take doses less than 3 hours apart.
- Allow at least 24 hours without Painkillers so accumulated dependency risk can begin to decrease.

Food-borne Diseases

🍕 Food Poisoning

Cause:
- Rotten food: high risk.
- Stale food: low to moderate risk.
- Burned food: low risk.
- Unsafe uncooked food can also cause it.
Typical timing:
- Incubation: 2-12 hours.
- Duration: 1-3 days.

Symptoms:
- Nausea.
- Vomiting.
- Endurance loss.
- Increased hunger and thirst.
- Weakness during peak stage.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - stomach feels wrong, mild sickness begins.
- Stage 2 - nausea becomes obvious, vomiting may begin.
- Stage 3 - worst stage; weakness and sickness are much stronger.
- Stage 4 - recovery; symptoms fade.

Treatment:
- Activated Charcoal cures Food Poisoning in about 6 hours.
- Homemade Activated Charcoal works the same way.
- Anti-Nausea Tablets reduce nausea and vomiting.
- Electrolyte Powder helps with dehydration and weakness.

Prevention:
- Avoid rotten, stale, burned, and unsafe uncooked food.

🦠 Gastroenteritis

Cause:
- Eating with bloody or dirty hands.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 4-12 hours.
- Duration: 1-3 days.

Symptoms:
- Nausea.
- Vomiting.
- Dehydration.
- Stomach discomfort.
- Fatigue and weakness.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - stomach feels off, appetite drops.
- Stage 2 - nausea and dehydration start becoming noticeable.
- Stage 3 - vomiting and weakness peak.
- Stage 4 - recovery; stomach settles.

Treatment:
- Antiviral Capsules cure it over a 48-hour course.
- IV Ciprofloxacin is the fast clinical treatment.
- Anti-Nausea Tablets reduce nausea and vomiting.
- Electrolyte Powder helps dehydration.

Prevention:
- Wash hands before eating.
- Soap and clean water are best.

🪱 Trichinosis

Cause:
- Raw or undercooked wild game and unsafe raw meat.
- Some risky meat types are much more dangerous than ordinary food poisoning.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 24-48 hours.
- Duration: 7-12 days.

Symptoms:
- Severe muscle pain.
- Fever.
- Weakness.
- Endurance loss.
- Movement penalty.
- Health damage in severe stages.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - incubation; little or no obvious symptoms.
- Stage 2 - stomach upset, feverish feeling, early muscle aches.
- Stage 3 - peak muscle pain, major movement and endurance problems, can become lethal.
- Stage 4 - recovery; pain fades but weakness can remain.

Treatment:
- Antiparasitic Pills cure it over a long course.
- Albendazole Injection is the fast clinical cure.
- Muscle Relaxants reduce muscle pain and spasms.
- Anti-Inflammatory Pills reduce pain and inflammation.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.

Prevention:
- Cook meat thoroughly.
- Be especially careful with wild game.

🍄 Toxin Poisoning

Cause:
- Poisonous mushrooms.
- Poisonous berries.
- Other food with poison/toxin values.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 1-4 hours.
- Duration: 1-4 days.

Symptoms:
- Severe nausea.
- Vomiting.
- Dizziness.
- Blurred vision.
- Weakness.
- Health damage in severe poisoning.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - bitter taste, early sickness.
- Stage 2 - nausea and dizziness increase.
- Stage 3 - dangerous poisoning; vomiting, weakness, and health damage become serious.
- Stage 4 - recovery if the poison is controlled.

Treatment:
- Activated Charcoal cures Toxin Poisoning in about 12 hours.
- Homemade Activated Charcoal works the same way.
- Anti-Nausea Tablets reduce nausea and vomiting.
- Electrolyte Powder helps dehydration and weakness.

Prevention:
- Do not eat unknown berries or mushrooms unless you can identify them.

🚽 Dysentery

Cause:
- Drinking contaminated water.
- Unsafe natural water sources.
- Toilet water has a high risk.
- Rain collectors and tainted containers can also be risky.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 6-12 hours.
- Duration: 3-5 days.

Main danger:
- Dysentery is dangerous mostly because of dehydration, not direct damage.

Symptoms:
- Diarrhea.
- Vomiting.
- Severe thirst.
- Hunger drain.
- Abdominal pain.
- Weakness.
- Some blood loss in later stages.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - mild gut symptoms; thirst and hunger are capped around a low level.
- Stage 2 - stronger dehydration and hunger drain, rare vomiting, abdominal pain, moderate blood loss.
- Stage 3 - dangerous dehydration, frequent vomiting, stronger abdominal pain, movement penalty, noticeable blood loss.
- Stage 4 - recovery; symptoms drop back to mild levels.

Treatment:
- Oral Rehydration Kit cures it over a 48-hour, 8-dose course.
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Plant-Based Antibiotics cure it over time.
- IV Antibiotics, IV Metronidazole, or IV Ciprofloxacin are clinical treatments.
- Anti-Diarrheal Tablets strongly reduce diarrhea and bathroom urgency.
- Electrolyte Powder helps dehydration.
- Anti-Nausea Tablets reduce vomiting.

Prevention:
- Boil or purify water.
- Avoid drinking from unsafe sources.

Environmental Diseases

🤧 Common Cold

Cause:
- Cold and wet exposure.
- Being very wet can trigger it even without extreme weather.
- Cold weather plus wetness makes it more likely.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 12-24 hours.
- Duration: 2-4 days.

Symptoms:
- Sneezing.
- Coughing.
- Mild fever.
- Fatigue.
- Reduced stamina.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - mild signs, occasional sneezing.
- Stage 2 - vanilla cold moodle appears, fatigue cap begins, sneezing is more frequent.
- Stage 3 - worst stage; stronger cold symptoms, fever, more frequent sneezing.
- Stage 4 - recovery, but if untreated there is a chance to progress into Pneumonia.

Treatment:
- Cold & Flu Tablets cure it over a 28-hour, 8-dose course.
- Antiviral Capsules can cure it over 48 hours.
- Cough Syrup and Cough Suppressant reduce coughing.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.

Prevention:
- Stay dry.
- Wear warm clothing in cold weather.
- Do not stay wet for long periods.

🫁 Pneumonia

Cause:
- Untreated Common Cold can progress into Pneumonia.
- Severe respiratory stress can also contribute.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 12-24 hours.
- Duration: 5-9 days.

Symptoms:
- Fever.
- Coughing.
- Severe cough in later stages.
- Chest pain.
- Endurance cap.
- Health damage in severe stages.
- Cough sounds can attract zombies.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - chest tightness, fever around 38 C, mild cough.
- Stage 2 - high fever, severe cough, chest pain, endurance capped around 70%.
- Stage 3 - same as Stage 2 plus health drain down to a dangerous cap.
- Stage 4 - severe late stage; health drain can become lethal without treatment.

Treatment:
- Prescription Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Plant-Based Antibiotics cure it over time.
- IV Ciprofloxacin is the fast clinical treatment.
- Bronchodilator Inhaler helps breathing and endurance.
- Cough Syrup and Cough Suppressant reduce coughing.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.
- Vanilla Antibiotics provide only minor help.

Prevention:
- Treat Common Cold early.
- Avoid staying wet and cold while sick.

❄️ Hypothermia

Cause:
- Body temperature dropping too low.
- Cold weather, wet clothing, wind, poor insulation, and exposure make it worse.

How it works:
- Hypothermia is driven by body temperature, not a normal timer.
- If you warm up, the stages can move backward.

Approximate stages:
- Stage 1 - around 35 C body temperature; shivering, mild slowdown, endurance loss.
- Stage 2 - around 34 C; cannot sprint, health begins draining.
- Stage 3 - around 32 C; severe movement penalty, confusion, blur/dizziness, heavy health drain.
- Stage 4 - around 31 C; collapse/blackouts and life-threatening health drain.

Treatment:
- Get indoors.
- Dry off.
- Change wet clothes.
- Use fire, shelter, insulation, and warm conditions.
- Medicine does not cure Hypothermia.

Prevention:
- Stay dry.
- Wear insulated clothing.
- Do not sleep or linger in freezing conditions.

♨️ Heat Exhaustion

Cause:
- Hot weather, especially around 30 C or higher.
- Prolonged exposure outside.
- Heavy clothing and exertion make it worse.

Progression:
- Heat exposure builds up over several in-game hours.
- Hats and head protection reduce buildup.
- Going indoors or cooling down reduces exposure.
- Untreated Heat Exhaustion can progress into Heat Stroke.

Symptoms:
- Thirst.
- Sweating.
- Cramps.
- Dizziness.
- Nausea.
- Weakness.
- Reduced movement and stamina.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - hot and thirsty.
- Stage 2 - cramps, stronger thirst, movement penalty.
- Stage 3 - dizziness, nausea, cannot sprint, strong heat stress.
- Stage 4 - recovery if cooled.

Treatment:
- Get indoors.
- Drink water.
- Remove heavy clothing.
- Electrolyte Powder helps.
- Oral Rehydration Kit can support recovery.

Prevention:
- Wear a hat in hot weather.
- Hydrate.
- Avoid long outdoor work during heat waves.

🥵 Heat Stroke

Cause:
- Untreated Heat Exhaustion.
- Extreme heat exposure and dehydration.

Typical timing:
- Immediate critical condition.
- Duration: 4-8 hours if survived.

Symptoms:
- Very high body temperature.
- Delirium/confusion.
- Dizziness and blurred vision.
- Collapse and forced sleep-like blackout.
- Heavy dehydration.
- Health drain.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - critical overheating, confusion, dizziness, collapse risk.
- Stage 2 - dehydration becomes dangerous and health starts draining.
- Stage 3 - peak danger; heavy health drain and frequent collapse.
- Stage 4 - still dangerous unless cooled.

Treatment:
- Instant Ice Pack cures it through a 4-dose cooling course.
- Cold bath treatment can cure it if available.
- Get in car and turn on air conditioner, and make sure it's <=10C (not showing as treatment). Also if you manage to get world temperature <=10C it's also considered as treatment
- Electrolyte Powder helps hydration but does not replace cooling.

Prevention:
- Treat Heat Exhaustion before it becomes Heat Stroke.
- Avoid staying in extreme heat while thirsty or exhausted.

Corpse Exposure Diseases

🧟‍♂️ Corpse Exposure Illness

Cause:
- Prolonged exposure to corpses.
- Enclosed spaces and indoor corpse piles are more dangerous.
- Rotten corpses are worse than fresh corpses.

Exposure:
- Low exposure starts around 30 exposure.
- Medium exposure starts around 60 exposure.
- High exposure starts around 100 exposure.
- Exposure decays when you leave the corpse area.
- Fresh air is the main counter.

Protection:
- Gas masks and proper respirators provide full protection when functional.
- Dust masks, surgical masks, bandanas, and cloth face coverings reduce exposure but do not always fully block it.
- Filter-based masks need working filters.

Symptoms:
- Eye irritation.
- Nausea.
- Dizziness.
- Weakness.
- Coughing.
- Collapse risk at high exposure.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - smell and mild nausea.
- Stage 2 - nausea and weakness.
- Stage 3 - coughing, headache, movement penalty.
- Stage 4 - recovery after fresh air or treatment.

Treatment:
- Leave the corpse area and get fresh air.
- Anti-Nausea Tablets reduce sickness.
- Bronchodilator Inhaler helps breathing.
- Respiratory Support Kit cures severe cases over a short 3-dose course.
- Corticosteroid Injection can treat severe inflammation but has side effects.

Prevention:
- Do not sleep or work near corpse piles.
- Remove corpses from buildings.
- Wear proper respiratory protection.

🧟‍♂️ Cadaveric Aspergillosis

Cause:
- Fungal spore exposure from corpses in damp, rainy, wet, or cold conditions.
- Damp corpse-filled indoor spaces are especially dangerous.
- Cold conditions count too.

Exposure:
- Builds separately from normal corpse sickness.
- Rain, wetness, cold air, and indoor damp conditions increase risk.
- Face protection reduces buildup.

Symptoms:
- Coughing.
- Fever.
- Respiratory irritation.
- Wheezing.
- Endurance loss.
- Weakness.
- Dehydration in severe stages.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - scratchy throat and mild chest irritation.
- Stage 2 - cough, feverish feeling, stamina loss.
- Stage 3 - severe respiratory symptoms, strong fatigue, no sprint, dangerous weakness.
- Stage 4 - recovery if treated.

Treatment:
- Antifungal Tablets cure it over a long course.
- IV Amphotericin B is the fast clinical cure.
- Bronchodilator Inhaler helps breathing.
- Cough Syrup and Cough Suppressant reduce coughing.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.

Prevention:
- Avoid damp corpse-filled areas.
- Wear face protection around corpses.
- Leave corpse areas during rain or cold damp weather.

Wound and Trauma Diseases

🩹 Wound Infection

Cause:
- Vanilla infected wounds.
- Dirty wounds.
- Poor wound care.
- Infected wounds can continue incubating even after the visible wound improves.

Typical timing:
- EHR begins tracking after detecting vanilla wound infection.
- EHR Wound Infection can appear after about 12 hours of incubation.

Symptoms:
- Local wound pain.
- Swelling/redness implied by the condition.
- Fever in later stages.
- Can progress to Sepsis if untreated.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - wound feels warm or tender.
- Stage 2 - infected wound becomes more painful.
- Stage 3 - fever and stronger pain; Sepsis risk becomes serious.
- Stage 4 - clearing/recovery if treated.

Treatment:
- Antibiotic Ointment can treat wound infection.
- Prescription Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Plant-Based Antibiotics cure it over time.
- IV Antibiotics are the fast clinical treatment.
- Anti-Inflammatory Pills reduce pain/inflammation.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.
- Antiseptic Cream helps prevent wound infection while active.

Prevention:
- Disinfect wounds.
- Use clean or sterile bandages.
- Replace dirty bandages quickly.
- Avoid closing dirty wounds.

🩹 Cellulitis

Cause:
- Spreading skin infection after a poorly closed wound.
- Rough stitching has a chance to trigger it.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 12-36 hours.
- Duration: 3-6 days.

Symptoms:
- Red, hot, swollen skin.
- Local pain.
- Fever.
- Fatigue.
- Weakness.
- Reduced endurance.
- Can progress into Sepsis at Stage 4.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - local redness and swelling.
- Stage 2 - redness spreads, pain and fever increase.
- Stage 3 - severe swelling and limb weakness.
- Stage 4 - systemic spread; Sepsis risk.

Treatment:
- Antibiotic Ointment can treat mild cases.
- Prescription Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Plant-Based Antibiotics cure it over time.
- IV Antibiotics or IV Vancomycin treat serious cases.
- Anti-Inflammatory Pills reduce pain and swelling.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.

Prevention:
- Clean wounds before stitching.
- Perform careful sutures.
- Use sterile supplies.

🩹 Sepsis

Cause:
- Severe untreated infection entering the bloodstream.
- Can develop from Wound Infection or Cellulitis.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 6-24 hours.
- Duration: 2-4 days.

Symptoms:
- High fever.
- Extreme weakness.
- Whole-body pain.
- Confusion.
- Rapid health decline.
- Can become fatal.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - feverish and weak.
- Stage 2 - whole body aches, shaking, serious infection symptoms.
- Stage 3 - septic shock, confusion, organ failure symptoms, lethal health drain.
- Stage 4 - recovery if treated, or continued danger if untreated.

Treatment:
- IV Antibiotics are a strong treatment.
- IV Vancomycin treats severe cases.
- Emergency Sepsis Kit is the fastest emergency treatment.
- Broad Spectrum Antibiotics cure it over time.
- Plant-Based Antibiotics cure it over time.
- IV Metronidazole can treat sepsis.
- Vanilla Antibiotics provide only minor help.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever but do not solve the infection.

Prevention:
- Treat Wound Infection and Cellulitis early.

🪡 Tetanus

Cause:
- Deep contaminated wounds.
- Glass in wounds.
- Dirty severe wounds.
- Walking on glass or car crash injuries can create the right conditions.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 3-7 days.
- Duration: 7-14 days or longer if untreated.

Symptoms:
- Jaw stiffness.
- Lockjaw.
- Muscle spasms.
- Neck and whole-body muscle pain.
- Fever.
- Breathing difficulty.
- Falling backward during spasms.
- Can prevent eating unless Muscle Relaxants are active.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - hidden incubation, little or no symptoms.
- Stage 2 - local tetanus: neck/jaw stiffness, difficulty eating, endurance cap, limited health damage.
- Stage 3 - generalized tetanus: full-body spasms, fever around 39 C, dangerous health drain, falls, breathing difficulty.
- Stage 4 - recovery if treatment is working.

Treatment:
- Tetanus Antitoxin cures over a 5-day course and requires a syringe.
- Tetanus Immunoglobulin is the fast clinical cure and requires a syringe.
- Muscle Relaxants reduce spasms and allow eating while active.
- Anti-Inflammatory Pills reduce pain.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.

Prevention:
- Clean deep wounds quickly.
- Remove glass.
- Do not ignore dirty deep wounds.

🤕 Concussion

Cause:
- Serious height falls, especially from high floors.
- Severe vehicle crashes.

How it works:
- Concussion starts at Stage 3 and improves over time.
- Total duration is about 48 hours.
- There is no direct cure.

Symptoms:
- Headache.
- Dizziness.
- Blurred vision.
- Severe nausea at first.
- Disorientation.
- Temporary health impact after the trauma.

Stages:
- Stage 3 - immediate severe stage: strong headache, blur, dizziness, nausea.
- Stage 2 - moderate stage: symptoms remain but are less intense.
- Stage 1 - mild stage: lingering headache and light nausea.
- Recovery - clears naturally with time.

Treatment:
- Rest and time.
- There is no direct EHR cure medication.

Prevention:
- Avoid high falls.
- Avoid high-speed crashes.

Parasitic and Skin Diseases

🪲 Hyperkeratotic Scabies

Cause:
- Sitting on natural ground such as dirt or grass 3% per 0.25 hour.
- Winter and snow block or strongly reduce this risk.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 0-2 hours.
- Duration: 4-7 days.

Symptoms:
- Itching.
- Bite-site pain.
- Repeated scratch wounds.
- Fever.
- Health decline in late stages.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - bite-site pain and itching.
- Stage 2 - fever around 38 C, health capped around 60%, scratches can appear every few hours.
- Stage 3 - fever around 40 C, health capped around 30%, scratches appear more often.
- Stage 4 - fever around 40 C and uncapped health damage.

Treatment:
- Topical Permethrin cures it over a 72-hour, 6-dose course.
- Antiparasitic Pills cure it over a longer course.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.

Prevention:
- Do not sit on bare natural ground for long periods.
- Snow/winter conditions are safer.

Blood and Transfusion Diseases

🩸 Acute Hemolytic Transfusion Reaction (AHTR)

Cause:
- Receiving incompatible blood.

Typical timing:
- Incubation: 1-6 hours after transfusion.
- Duration: 2-4 days.

Symptoms:
- Lower back pain.
- Severe weakness.
- Nausea.
- High fever.
- Endurance loss.
- Health drain.
- Blood reaction/hemolysis.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - lower back ache, sudden weakness, early reaction.
- Stage 2 - fever around 40 C, nausea, endurance cap, health drain capped around 50%.
- Stage 3 - severe reaction, health drain becomes uncapped and dangerous.
- Stage 4 - late critical stage unless stabilized.

Treatment:
- IV Fluids are the main clinical support treatment.
- Furosemide treats over a 48-hour, 6-dose course.
- Anti-Nausea Tablets reduce nausea.
- Anti-Inflammatory Pills reduce pain.
- Antipyretic Tablets reduce fever.

Prevention:
- Use compatible blood.
- Learn blood type compatibility from the Blood Type flyer or reach First Aid 8.

Mental Conditions

🤪 Delirium

Cause:
- Stress held at maximum for about 12 hours.

Duration:
- Permanent until treated.

Symptoms:
- Hallucinated sounds.
- Strange speech.
- Screen tint/visual distortion.
- Unsafe impulses.
- The character may break nearby windows, scream, drop a worn backpack, or fire a held firearm.

Stages:
- Delirium has one persistent stage.

Treatment:
- Antipsychotics cure it over a 96-hour, 8-dose course.

Prevention:
- Do not allow stress to stay at maximum for long periods.

😴 Insomnia

Cause:
- Fatigue above 80% for about 12 hours.
- Small chance after stimulant crash from Caffeine Pills, Combat Stimulants, or Nitric Oxide Boosters.

Progression:
- Stage 1 starts first.
- Stage 2 begins after about 24 hours.
- Stage 3 begins after about 48 hours and does not naturally recover.

Symptoms:
- Sleep stops helping.
- Later stages can block natural sleep.
- Stress.
- Fatigue.
- Headache.

Stages:
- Stage 1 - poor rest; sleep does not recover you properly.
- Stage 2 - exhausted but unable to fall asleep naturally.
- Stage 3 - persistent insomnia; requires treatment to recover.

Treatment:
- Sleeping Pills allow sleep while active, but do not cure Insomnia.
- Dual Orexin Receptor medication cures over a 96-hour, 8-dose course and allows sleep while active.
- Antidepressants cure over a 168-hour, 14-dose course and also act as a sleep aid while active.

Prevention:
- Sleep before fatigue stays extreme for too long.
- Avoid stimulant abuse.

🦝 Knox Infection

Cause:
- Vanilla zombie infection mechanics.
- Usually from infected wounds such as bites.

Knowledge:
- Knox Infection is unknown by default, even for medical professions.
- The disease card is revealed by reading the Kentucky Herald July 16, 1993 newspaper.

Symptoms:
- Controlled mostly by vanilla Knox rules.
- Fever.
- Nausea.
- Weakness.
- Deterioration.
- Eventual death and zombification if not cured by special systems.

Stages:
- Stage display follows infection progression, but the actual infection behavior is handled by vanilla mechanics.

Treatment:
- There is no normal medical cure.
- Gene Therapy Injector can remove Knox if compatibility succeeds.
- Compatibility depends on blood type compatibility.
- Antibody Compatibility Test reveals the odds.
- Immunobooster Shot can provide temporary protection.
- If sandbox settings allow it, successful Gene Therapy can grant Patient Zero immunity.

Prevention:
- Avoid bites.
- Use Immunobooster Shot before exposure if available.
