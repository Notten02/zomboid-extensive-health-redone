This is first implementation of this system so it may be unbalanced or lack of details. I will expand this mechanic in the future if there requests for it.

Immune System Status updates every 15 in game minutes. It has target point and current point. Depending of where target point is - Immune System will increase or decrease

What Affects Immunity?
Nutrition and hydration
Fatigue and quality of rest
Stress
Current blood volume
Overall health and active diseases
Clothing and body dirtiness
Clothing and body bloodiness
Active systemic antibiotics

Dirtiness and bloodiness are especially important. Staying clean, rested, hydrated, and well-fed helps maintain a strong immune response.

Immune Status
Strong: 80–100%
Stable: 60–79%
Strained: 40–59%
Compromised: 20–39%
Suppressed: 0–19%

At maximum strength, immunity can reduce supported risks by up to approximately 35%. Very poor immunity may increase those risks by up to approximately 50%. The exact effect depends on the disease.

Supported Diseases
Common Cold
Pneumonia

Gastroenteritis
Cadaveric Aspergillosis
Tuberculosis
Hyperkeratotic Scabies
Cellulitis
EHR Wound Infection incubation

A strong immune system may contain an incubating wound infection before it develops. It cannot remove an infection that has already become active.

Antibiotic Suppression
Systemic antibiotics temporarily suppress the immune system while their dose is active.
Immunity gradually falls toward 35%
It decreases by 4 points every 15 in-game minutes
Recovery begins normally after the antibiotic effect ends

Antibiotic ointments and other topical treatments do not suppress immunity
The active penalty is displayed under Current Pressures as Antibiotic Suppression.

Important Notes
Immunity does not affect every disease.
It does not stop exposure accumulation.
It does not alter active symptoms or disease progression.
It does not replace medication, wound care, hygiene, or protective equipment.
